# MeterHover

## 1.2.0

First release on CurseForge and Wago. Since 1.0.0:

- Share line under the preview: pet and DoT shares (Damage Done, DPS), Drain Life and
  Health Funnel (Healing), top attacker (Damage Taken)
- Fight summary when you hover a fight in the meter's session menu
- Settings panel (Esc > Options > AddOns > MeterHover, also the addon compartment), with an
  open delay slider; `/meterhover delay`, `share`, `summary`, `help`
- Shows in the AddOns list under Meio, with a spyglass icon

## 1.0.0

- First version: hover a damage meter row to preview its breakdown
